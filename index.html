Portraits Rooted in Feeling, Light, and Place

Irina makes photographs that feel intimate, cinematic, and alive: images with breath, texture, and a quiet sense of wonder.

Her eye is drawn to living texture: leaves at the edge of a face, saltwater on skin, a child moving through sun, cloth catching wind, a room holding the trace of someone loved. She does not rush people into poses. She gives the moment enough space to become honest.

Whether she is photographing a family, an artist, a maker, or an editorial story, the feeling is the same: grounded, luminous, and deeply personal. Moss & Tea is for people who want photographs with soul, not polish alone.

About

I make images that feel like memory before they become photographs.

I am Irina Crawford, the photographer behind Moss & Tea. I work quietly, with patience and attention to what is already alive: the way someone softens when they feel safe, the gesture between words, the light that finds a face for only a second.

I come from a filmmaking eye, so I think in atmosphere, movement, and emotional rhythm. A session with me is not about performing a perfect version of yourself. It is about entering a small world together and letting the real image rise.

Based in California and available for travel, I work with families, artists, makers, and brands who want photographs with tenderness, strangeness, beauty, and a living pulse.
