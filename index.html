About

I see the world through a lens of light and story.

I am Irina Crawford — a visual storyteller, cinematographer, and photographer. My work lives at the intersection of the natural world and the intimate moments that define us. With roots in filmmaking and an eye for the cinematic, every frame I capture is a meditation on light, texture, and emotion.

From the olive groves of the Mediterranean to the misty forests of the Pacific Northwest, I seek the poetry in the ordinary — the way morning light falls across a table, the quiet strength in a portrait, the wild grace of an untamed landscape.

Based between California and the world, I work with families, creators, and brands who value authenticity over perfection.
