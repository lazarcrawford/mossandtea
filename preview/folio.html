The Held Gaze

A Note from the Author

I come from a filmmaking eye.

I think in atmosphere, movement, and emotional rhythm. A session with me is not about performing a perfect version of yourself. It is about entering a small world together and letting the real image rise.

Based in California and available for travel, I work with families, artists, makers, and brands who want photographs with tenderness, strangeness, beauty, and a living pulse.

Four Ways I Work

I

Portrait & Family

Tender, unforced portraits. Quiet direction, natural movement, and room for real feeling.

II

Lifestyle & Editorial

Cinematic stories for artists, publications, and creative projects.

III

Nature & Place

Landscape, water, earth, and atmosphere for projects that need the natural world to feel present and alive.

IV

Brand Stories

Visual storytelling for makers who want their work to feel human, textured, and emotionally specific.
