The Photographer

I make images that feel like memory before they become photographs.

I work quietly, with patience and attention to what is already alive: the way someone softens when they feel safe, the gesture between words, the light that finds a face for only a second.

I come from a filmmaking eye, so I think in atmosphere, movement, and emotional rhythm. Based in California, available for travel.
