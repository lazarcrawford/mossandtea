DesignMD Visual Reference

Cinematic, earthy, editorial.

A human-facing guide to the Moss & Tea design language: warm neutrals, restrained typography, quiet utility, and photography that carries the emotional weight.

Color Tokens

Warm surfaces, clay accents, sage restraint.

The palette should read as photographic and organic, not themed. Cream and charcoal do most of the work; clay and sage are accents.

Typography

Elegant display type, quiet utility type.

Cormorant Garamond brings the editorial mood. Inter keeps forms, labels, navigation, and admin work clear.

Body MD

Portraits and stories should feel intimate rather than promotional. Body copy stays light, long-lined, and breathable so the photography remains the center of attention.

Imagery

Images are the interface.

Use real photography as the primary signal. Captions and overlays support context without interrupting the image.

Components

Simple shapes, restrained states.

Components should frame the work, not announce themselves. Corners are mostly square on the public site; admin can use modest 8px radii for utility.

Primary action

Charcoal fill, uppercase Inter, generous spacing. Clay appears on hover.

Principles

The aesthetic in five decisions.

Photography leads Images carry the emotional weight.
Editorial restraint Whitespace, thin borders, quiet hierarchy.
Earth and light Warm neutrals, clay, sage, charcoal.
Cinematic pacing Large first impressions and slow reveals.
Operational calm Admin is denser and more practical.

Admin Interpretation

A quiet studio command center.

Admin screens should inherit the brand without becoming precious. They need Inter, tighter spacing, clear status, and fewer decorative choices.
